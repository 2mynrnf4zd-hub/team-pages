To do for families

What the parents' page asks families to do, soonest due first.

  1. Sign the Player/Parent Commitment Contract
    Read the club's contract, then have the player and a parent or guardian sign and date the last page. Give it to the coaches at the first practice.
    Due at the first practice, Mon Nov 30
    The contract ↗
  2. Identify tournament dates you cannot attend
    Please use the "not attending" button below to identify any tournaments your daughter will be missing.
    As soon as possible
  3. Complete the USAV Junior Referee trainings
    Every player completes GE27_102 New and Renewing Junior Second Referee and GE27_103 New and Renewing Junior Scorer: sign in to SportsEngine, launch the USA Volleyball Academy under the player's profile, and find them on its homepage or in the Content Library. They show 50% when finished; USAV marks them 100% later. The player needs the GEVA Junior Competition Player membership and the Big City club assignment first.
    Due December 2
    SportsEngine sign-in ↗ GEVA membership and club assignment ↗
  4. Book tournament hotels
    See below for hotel booking links and deadlines.
